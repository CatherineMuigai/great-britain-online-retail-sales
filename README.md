# Great Britain online retail sales

Which types of retailers drove the increase in online sales between October 2025 and October 2026?

Average weekly online sales rose from £2,655.6 million to £2,921.4 million, an increase of £265.8 million (10.0%). Non-store retailers contributed £173.7 million, or 65.3% of the increase. Household goods grew the fastest at 22.2% and added £39.9 million.

## How the analysis works

The workbook compares the same month in consecutive years using the Office for National Statistics' non-seasonally adjusted series. Sales are measured in pounds at current prices and exclude automotive fuel. Each observation is an average week within the reporting month.

The analysis compares both the amount and percentage by which each category changed. The amount shows how much a category contributed to overall growth. The percentage shows how fast the category grew relative to its own starting point. The six category changes sum to the published overall change.

Open the [Excel workbook](Great%20Britain%20Online%20Retail%20Sales.xlsx) to see the formulas and editable chart on **Overview**. **Monthly Data** contains 240 observations, from November 2006 through October 2026, with the ONS series identifiers.
